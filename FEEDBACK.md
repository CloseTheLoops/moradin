# If you've tried the forge — five minutes, please

Blunt beats polite. Rough notes are fine.

## The main thing

Did the pipeline make building your thing better, or did it put steps between you and the thing? If the second — at what exact moment did you feel it?

## The stages

- Which stage did you skip or rush every time? (That stage is probably wrong.)
- Did the calibration match how you actually work, or did the process still feel one-size?
- The Success Contract: did agreeing on a kill line up front change anything later — or was it theater?
- Did you trust the research pages? Did you catch them being wrong?

## The clicking

- The interactive pages (contract, decisions): better than answering in chat, worse, or the same?
- Did the export-paste-back loop ever break or feel silly?

## The honest ones

- Did anything catch a mistake you'd have shipped?
- Would you start your next real project with `/moradin:forge` if nobody was watching?
- If you could delete one stage forever, which one goes?
